# Hardware Requirements

## Overview

This course on Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics is technically demanding as it sits at the intersection of three heavy computational loads: Physics Simulation (Isaac Sim/Gazebo), Visual Perception (SLAM/Computer Vision), and Generative AI (LLMs/VLA). The computational requirements for this course are substantial and require careful hardware planning to ensure a smooth learning experience.

## The Computational Challenge

The combination of simulation, perception, and AI workloads creates unique challenges:

- **Physics Simulation**: Requires high-performance CPUs for rigid body dynamics calculations
- **Visual Perception**: Demands powerful GPUs for real-time computer vision and SLAM
- **Generative AI**: Needs GPUs with significant VRAM for running large language models and neural networks
- **Real-time Control**: Requires consistent performance for robot control and response

## Hardware Categories

The hardware requirements can be divided into three main categories:

### 1. The "Digital Twin" Workstation
The critical computational component for simulation and development

### 2. The "Physical AI" Edge Kit
Hardware for deploying AI systems to physical robots

### 3. The Robot Lab
Physical robotic platforms for testing and deployment

Each category serves a specific purpose in the learning process and can be implemented at different budget levels.
